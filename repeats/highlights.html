Research Highlight · 2024

LLM-Guided Discovery of Protein Repeat Motifs

A screen applying large language model reasoning to discover short linear motifs (SLiMs) and IDR motifs in human proteins — capturing structured repeat architectures that traditional bioinformatics tools miss. Live UniProt annotations and AlphaFold structures are loaded for each protein.

About this screen

Traditional repeat-detection tools require sequence similarity — they miss motifs that vary between copies, the hallmark of real SLiMs in IDRs. We prompted a large language model with protein sequences and asked it to reason about recurring structural or functional patterns, generating a consensus notation for each discovery. All 15 candidates shown here were manually curated against UniProt and AlphaFold structures. The full dataset of 2,271 screened proteins with 1,124 predicted-repeat proteins is available in the interactive curation tool.
